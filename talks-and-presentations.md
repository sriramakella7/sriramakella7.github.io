- ***Genuine multiparty entanglement in stabilizer states and topology.*** (Sep 30, 2026) I gave this talk at the [Taiwan String Workshop 2026](https://phys.ncts.ntu.edu.tw/en/act/actnews/Taiwan%20String%20Workshop%202026%20-96162504/home/introduction), National Tsing Hua University, Hsinchu.

- ***Genuine Multi-entropy in the toric code.*** (Jul 30, 2026) Blackboard talk at the Yukawa Institute of Theoretical Physics, Kyoto University as part of their weekly seminar. Mostly discussed the [toric code paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06050) with Iizuka-san and Miyata-san.

- ***ST4 Lectures on Quantum Information and Holography.*** (Jun 22 - Jul 4, 2026) Lecture series at the [ST$^4$ workshop](https://sites.google.com/view/st4-2026/home) meant for advanced graduate students and postdoctoral researchers from India. Introduced holographic error-correcting codes and tensor network toy models of holography, along with guided exercises. (The notes of these lectures will be available soon on arXiv.)

- ***Tripartite entanglement in the HaPPY code is not holographic.*** (Dec 4, 2025) Talk at the [Taiwan String Workshop 2025](https://www.google.com/search?hl=en&q=taiwan%20string%20workshop%202025), National Tsing Hua University, Hsinchu. The slides are available [here](/assets/taiwan-talk-slides-2025.pdf).

- ***Virasoro TQFT as a description of pure 3D gravity.*** (Aug 21, 2025) Talk at TIFR as part of our group's weekly virtual tea seminar. I discussed some recent developments in 3d gravity based on [arXiv:2304.13650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.13650). Slides available [here](/assets/Virasoro_TQFT.pdf).
