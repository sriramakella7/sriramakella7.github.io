Hello and welcome to my website!

I'm Sriram Akella, a Ph.D. candidate at the Department of Theoretical Physics, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai, India. I broadly work on Quantum Information Theory, Quantum Gravity, and Quantum Field Theory. My Ph.D. work, under Prof. Abhijit Gadde's supervision, focused on studying multipartite entanglement and what it reveals about topological phases of matter, quantum error-correcting codes, and holographic spacetimes. I'm currently exploring quantum nonlocal magic and what it diagnoses in holographic and quantum many-body systems.
